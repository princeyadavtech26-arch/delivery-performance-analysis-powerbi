# 📦 Delivery Performance Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The Delivery Performance Analysis Dashboard is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI and the DataCo Supply Chain dataset. It analyzes delivery delays, late orders, product categories, shipping modes, and regional performance to identify areas for improvement in supply chain operations.

## 🎯 Business Question

**Which product categories, shipping modes, and regions experience delivery delays, and what actions can a company take to improve delivery performance?**

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- DataCo Supply Chain Dataset

## 📄 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overall Delivery Performance

Provides an overview of order delivery performance using KPI cards and charts.

**Key Metrics:**
- Total Orders: 65,752
- Late Orders: 36,048
- Late Delivery Percentage: 54.8%
- Average Actual Shipping Days: 3.5
- Average Scheduled Shipping Days: 2.9

**Key Insights:**
- Western Europe has the highest late-order count among the displayed regions, with 5,585 late orders.
- Standard Class has the highest late-order count among shipping modes, with 14,995 late orders.
- Average actual shipping time exceeds average scheduled shipping time.

### 2. Product & Shipping Analysis

Analyzes late-delivery rates across product categories and shipping modes, along with late-order counts by product category.

**Key Insights:**
- Golf Bags & Carts has the highest displayed late-delivery rate among the selected categories, at 68.9%.
- Cleats has the highest displayed late-order count among the selected categories, at 11,228.
- First Class has the highest displayed late-delivery rate among shipping modes, at 95%.
- Standard Class has the lowest displayed late-delivery rate among shipping modes, at 38%.

### 3. Delivery Delay Investigation & Business Recommendations

Examines average shipping delay across shipping modes and regions, along with state-level late-delivery percentages.

**Key Insights:**
- Second Class has the highest displayed average shipping delay, at approximately 1.99 days.
- First Class has an average shipping delay of approximately 1.00 day.
- South Asia and Western Europe show average shipping delays of approximately 0.60 days in the regional chart.
- State-level late-delivery percentages help identify locations for further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **Shipping Mode Optimization:** Investigate the reasons for higher average delays in Second Class shipping by reviewing order processing, dispatch, and transit performance.

2. **Regional Performance Improvement:** Compare late-order volume, late-delivery percentage, and average shipping delay to identify regions requiring operational attention.

3. **Product Category Monitoring:** Investigate the high late-delivery rate for Golf Bags & Carts and the high late-order count for Cleats.

4. **State-Level Investigation:** Review order volumes alongside late-delivery percentages before prioritizing states for improvement.

5. **Performance Tracking:** Monitor late-delivery percentage, average shipping delay, and late-order volume to evaluate future improvements.

## 📈 Business Value

This dashboard helps demonstrate how data analytics can support supply chain decision-making by:

- Monitoring delivery performance through KPIs.
- Identifying regions and shipping modes with delivery challenges.
- Highlighting product categories with high late-delivery rates.
- Comparing late-order volume with late-delivery percentage.
- Supporting data-driven operational recommendations.
